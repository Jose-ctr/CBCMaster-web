📚 CBC PROJECTS

Practical projects designed to help learners research, create and present their work.

🌱 Project 1 — School Garden

Create a small school or home garden and record the plants grown.

  • Identify at least three plants.
  • Record watering and growth.
  • Explain why plants are important.
  • Present findings to the class.

💧 Project 2 — Water Conservation

Investigate how water is used at home or school and propose ways to reduce wastage.

  • Observe water use.
  • Identify sources of wastage.
  • Suggest practical solutions.
  • Prepare a short presentation.

Research 5

♻️ Project 3 — Waste Management

Study waste around the home or school and create a simple waste-separation plan.

  • Identify different types of waste.
  • Separate recyclable and non-recyclable materials.
  • Explain safe disposal.
  • Create a poster or model.
